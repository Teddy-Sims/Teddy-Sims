# Professional Bio

Hi, I’m Teddy Sims. I’m an operations and business professional who enjoys figuring out how people, processes, and systems can work better together. My background has taken me through military leadership, transportation and service operations, financial services, human resources, and small-business operations, which has given me the chance to see organizations from many different angles.

I’m a U.S. Army veteran and former officer, and much of my leadership style comes from learning how to support people while still getting the mission done. I enjoy fast-paced environments, solving messy problems, improving inefficient processes, and turning big ideas into practical plans that people can actually execute.

I’m currently pursuing my Global MBA at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa’s Shidler College of Business. I’m especially interested in operations management, business analytics, strategy, supply chain and logistics, process improvement, and organizational leadership.

What excites me most is learning how data, technology, and strong leadership can work together to build better organizations. I’m naturally curious, I love learning new things, and I’m always looking for opportunities to challenge myself and grow.

Long term, I hope to continue developing into a strategic business leader who can see the big picture while still understanding what it takes to make things work on the ground.
